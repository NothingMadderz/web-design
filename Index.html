About SUNY New Paltz

A public university combining strong academics, creative opportunities, and a beautiful Hudson Valley setting.

Learn. Create. Discover.

SUNY New Paltz offers students opportunities to explore their interests while developing the knowledge and skills needed for life after college.

Located in the Hudson Valley, the campus combines a traditional college environment with access to a vibrant surrounding community and the natural beauty of the region.

Students can participate in academic programs, student organizations, athletics, arts, research, internships, and many other activities.
